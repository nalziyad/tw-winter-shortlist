Revel's terms, from the brochure

Included

  • Tomorrowland Winter Festival Pass, General Access (Comfort is an upgrade)
  • 7 nights: check-in 20 March 16:00, check-out 27 March 10:00
  • 7-day lift & ski pass, Alpe d'Huez Grand Domaine, Sat 20 to Fri 26 March

Not included

  • Airfare, visa, medical insurance

  • Non-refundable. One name change allowed before 16 January 2027.
  • Prices use 1 EUR = AED 4.33 and can rise if the rate moves.
  • Card or payment-link payments cost 3% extra; bank transfer does not.
  • Comfort upgrade €390 pp, subject to availability. Booking needs passport copies.
  • Festival pass value inside the package: €705 pp (ski pass €389 + 7-day ticket €316).
