Mountain stages by day are reached from two lifts: the Marmottes gondola at Les Bergers (two stages) and the Signal lift at Rond-Point des Pistes; each card shows the walk to both. Night stages, including the Mainstage, are entered only via the roundabout in front of Les Bergers, so "walk to night entrance" is the distance that matters after dark: a straight-line estimate with a 30% detour factor at 80 m/min.
